L2-L3 SF Giants fan in Milwaukee

I always was a SF Giants fan because that's the area I'm from, but never truly enjoyed baseball or went to a game until I lived in Milwaukee. Going to Brewers home games eventually would become one of my favorite activities to do with friends activity I really enjoyed to do.

For the second half of the world series, thousands of milwaukee fans flooded the stands of in Florida in support of their team. I truly never been around sports fans that are as loyal as the fans who show up to American Family field and get invested in the game. It feels like I am in a comfortable spot that you forget you're watching some of the worlds best mlb players. SF Giants have won their share of world series, but experiencing this with the most deserving fans is what really caught my attention about the strong connection between The Brewers and Milwaukee.

Watch Parties

Over 30,000 fans sold out a stadium just for a watch party when The Brewers played the second half in Florida. "It's time for Milwaukee to have a World Series"(TMJ4 news) was a fans response when asked about the Brewers winning. Another family had to experience it for themselves and traveled from California to witness the win of their team.

I went to game 4 for the watch party and it was a crazy atmosphere to be around. This was busier then any regular season game i've been to and they weren't physically playing there.

L4-L5 Win Our Way

All year leading up to thWorld Series The Brewers were putting stats on the board with high batting averages and on base percentages. They then repeated it again for the postseason. It was only a matter of time that they went to the top of the mountain.

When The Brewers won the world series, "Win our way" became the slogan for the city. This slogan fit because the Brewers did just that when they didn't rely on "one fixed formula". They finished the world series and the season with a 103-59 record which is the best record in the MLB.

The city of Milwaukee knows what it is like to be seen as an underdog and not be seen as a favorite. That is one similarity that the team and the city have and I believe is what fueled each other

City Parade

I was able to attend The Brewers parade and as well as an estimated 750,000 people. 400,000 people were estimated, but this number nearly doubled. The streets were jam packed with fans piling into parking structures, apartment buildings, and anywhere they could celebrate with their team.

During the day Milwaukee Brewers players and staff showed their appreciation to the fans by hanging out with them at local restaurants, taking photos on the street and at local food markets. It really felt like a "we did it" moment between a city and the team

Meaning to the city

For 57 years Milwaukee Brewers fans showed their patience as they showed their support through the rough years and the good years. Now they get to say that it was all worth the wait.

Pat Murphy,The Brewers head coach took a notice to the fans traveling from all across the state and saw their commitments to the team. This was never a one sided relationship

I am a San Francisco Giants fan because I have always been, but I have a deep appreciation for The Milwaukee Brewers because they had the best year in the MLB history and they have the best fans to back them up from the rough years to winning the World Series.
